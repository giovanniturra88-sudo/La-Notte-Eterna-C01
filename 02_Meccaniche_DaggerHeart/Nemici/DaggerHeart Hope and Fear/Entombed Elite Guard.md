# Entombed Elite Guard
- **Tipo / Ruolo:** Tier 2 Standard
- **Descrizione:** An elite undead soldier buried with their empress to defend the tomb from theft and desecration.
- **Motivi & Tattiche:** Form up, protect the empress, strike as one
- **Difficoltà:** 15
- **Soglie (Thresholds):** 9 / 18
- **Punti Ferita (HP):** 4
- **Stress:** 3
- **Attacco:** +2 | Curved Blade: Melee | 2d10+2 phy
- **Esperienza:** Soldier +3

**Tratti (Features):**
* **Battle Formation - Passive:** The Guard gains a +1 bonus to their Difficulty for each ally within Very Close range.
* **Javelin - Action:** Make an attack against a target within Very Far range. On a success, deal 2d6+2 physical damage.
