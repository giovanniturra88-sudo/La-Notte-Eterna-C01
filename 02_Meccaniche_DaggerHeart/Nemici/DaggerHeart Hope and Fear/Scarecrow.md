# Scarecrow
- **Tipo / Ruolo:** Tier 2 Standard
- **Descrizione:** A bundle of straw bound into a humanoid shape, clothed in rags, and animated by a trapped soul.
- **Motivi & Tattiche:** Pull yourself together, scratch and claw
- **Difficoltà:** 15
- **Soglie (Thresholds):** 7 / 15
- **Punti Ferita (HP):** 4
- **Stress:** 3
- **Attacco:** +1 | Sickle: Melee | 2d6+1 phy
- **Esperienza:** Birds +2, Fields +2

**Tratti (Features):**
* **Made of Straw - Passive:** The Scarecrow has resistance to physical damage but takes double damage from fire.
* **Terror Vision - Action:** Spend a Fear to have the Scarecrow lock eyes with a PC within Very Close range, dealing 2d8 magic damage and making them Vulnerable until they roll with Hope.
