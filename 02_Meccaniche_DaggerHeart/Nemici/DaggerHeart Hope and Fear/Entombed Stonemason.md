# Entombed Stonemason
- **Tipo / Ruolo:** Tier 2 Minion
- **Descrizione:** An unfortunate undead laborer chosen by lottery to wall up their empress's tomb from the inside.
- **Motivi & Tattiche:** Never stop working, sacrifice yourself for the empress
- **Difficoltà:** 12
- **Soglie (Thresholds):** None
- **Punti Ferita (HP):** 1
- **Stress:** 1
- **Attacco:** -1 | Hammer and Chisel: Melee | 5 phy
- **Esperienza:** Builder +2

**Tratti (Features):**
* **Minion (5) - Passive:** The Stonemason is defeated when they take any damage. For every 5 damage a PC deals to the Stonemason, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range their attack would succeed against.
* **Group Attack - Action:** Spend a Fear to choose a target and spotlight all Entombed Stonemasons within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 5 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
* **The Work Never Ends - Reaction:** When the Stonemason is defeated by an attack that deals physical damage, you can spend a Fear to clear the Stonemason's HP and Stress.
