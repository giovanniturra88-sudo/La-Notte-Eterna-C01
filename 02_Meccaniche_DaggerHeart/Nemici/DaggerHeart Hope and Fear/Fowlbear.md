# Fowlbear
- **Tipo / Ruolo:** Tier 2 Bruiser
- **Descrizione:** A grizzly bear with a feathery hide and the head of a giant goose.
- **Motivi & Tattiche:** Drive prey into an ambush, fight to the death, hunt in pairs
- **Difficoltà:** 15
- **Soglie (Thresholds):** 14 / 28
- **Punti Ferita (HP):** 7
- **Stress:** 4
- **Attacco:** +3 | Goose Teeth & Bear Claws: Very Close | 2d12+2 phy
- **Esperienza:** Keen Senses +3, Ponds +3

**Tratti (Features):**
* **Relentless (2) - Passive:** The Fowlbear can be spotlighted up to two times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight it.
* **Double Strike - Action:** Mark a Stress to make two standard attacks. If both attacks succeed against the same target, combine the damage.
* **Dread Honk - Action:** Spend a Fear to have the Fowlbear unleash a terrifying, ear-splitting goose honk. Each PC within Close range must make a Presence Reaction Roll. Targets who fail must mark a Stress, move away from the Fowlbear to Far range, and become Vulnerable until the Fowlbear deals damage to them. Targets who succeed must mark a Stress or move away from the Fowlbear to Far range.
