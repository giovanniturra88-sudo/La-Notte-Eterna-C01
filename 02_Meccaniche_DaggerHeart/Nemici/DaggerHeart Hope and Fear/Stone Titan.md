# Stone Titan
- **Tipo / Ruolo:** Tier 2 Solo
- **Descrizione:** A gray-skinned goliath who lives underground and specializes in crafting powerful items inlaid with magical runes.
- **Motivi & Tattiche:** Blend into the rocks, craft and contemplate, pursue prophecy and solitude
- **Difficoltà:** 17
- **Soglie (Thresholds):** 17 / 32
- **Punti Ferita (HP):** 5
- **Stress:** 5
- **Attacco:** +3 | Stone Hammer: Very Close | 3d6+8 phy
- **Esperienza:** Crafting +3, Runes +3

**Tratti (Features):**
* **Relentless (3) - Passive:** The Titan can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them.
* **Skillful Strike - Passive:** Before the Titan makes an attack, you can mark a Stress and roll a d6. On a result of 3 or higher, the target's Evasion is halved against this attack.
* **Sunlight Sickness - Passive:** When the Titan first appears above ground, roll a d4. Mark a number of Stress equal to the result. The Titan gains a bonus to their attack and damage rolls equal to the number of Stress marked.
* **Hammer Smash - Action:** When a PC marks HP from the Titan's standard attack, you can spend a Fear to force the target to mark an Armor Slot without gaining its benefits (they can still use armor to reduce the damage).
