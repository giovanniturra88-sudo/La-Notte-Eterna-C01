# Time Court
*Tier 4 Event*
*One or more PCs are forcibly plucked from the timeline and put on trial for crimes against continuity.*

**Impulses:** Find the truth, mete out justice, nullify their powers[cite: 4]
**Difficulty:** 20[cite: 4]
**Potential Adversaries:** Judge (Monarch), Jury (Hallowed Choir), Executioners (Temporal Enforcers)[cite: 4]

## FEATURES
* **Out of Time - Passive:** The trial takes place in a pocket dimension that exists beyond the influence of the other realms[cite: 4]. While here, PCs can't use their features and abilities[cite: 4]. Each player must put their Domain cards in their vault[cite: 4].
  * *What besides the courtroom resides in this pocket dimension? What powers maintain the universal barrier protecting this place?*[cite: 4]
* **Trial by Jury - Passive:** At the end of the proceedings, a jury votes whether to acquit or convict the party[cite: 4]. When the court is called to order, have the prosecution present its case against the PCs and start a Countdown (9)[cite: 4]. It ticks down when a PC makes an action roll[cite: 4]. When it triggers, the PCs and GM roll their Judgment Dice[cite: 4]. If the party's total result meets or exceeds the GM's, the jury acquits them[cite: 4].
  * *Which PCs are put on trial? What are they accused of? What potential penalties would a guilty verdict bring?*[cite: 4]
* **Counsel for the Defense - Passive:** A PC can make an action roll to argue for their innocence (such as a Knowledge Roll to interpret the letter of the law, a Presence Roll to play on the jury's emotions, or an Instinct Roll to point out holes in the prosecution's case)[cite: 4]. On a success, the party gains a d6 Judgment Die[cite: 4]. On a critical success, they gain two[cite: 4]. On a failure, the prosecution rebuts the PC's argument, and you gain a d6 Judgment Die[cite: 4].
  * *What is the weakest part of the prosecution's case? What is the strongest part of the party's defense?*[cite: 4]
* **Counsel for the Prosecution - Action:** The prosecution introduces a damning piece of evidence (such as an eyewitness testimony, test results, or a physical exhibit taken from the scene of the crime), and you gain a d6 Judgment Die[cite: 4]. A PC can mark a Stress to attempt a rebuttal of the evidence with a Knowledge, Instinct, or Presence Reaction Roll[cite: 4]. On a success, the party gains the Judgment Die instead of you[cite: 4]. On a failure, you gain an additional d6 Judgment Die[cite: 4].
  * *Who leads the prosecution, and what is their relationship to the Time Council? What magic or technology does this court rely on to present and record evidence?*[cite: 4]
* **"Order in the Court!" - Reaction:** When the PCs attempt to escape or end the trial through force, summon a number of Temporal Enforcers equal to the number of PCs in the scene within Close range and immediately spotlight one[cite: 4].
  * *Is this court's authority worthy of respect? What besides the might of the Time Council and its enforcers justifies their control over the timeline?*[cite: 4]
