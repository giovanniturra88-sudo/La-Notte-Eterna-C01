# Volcanic Eruption
*Tier 3 Event*
*An explosion of heat and molten rock that leaves behind only charred desolation.*

**Impulses:** Create panic all around, cut off the exits, isolate, make them scramble for safety, split the party[cite: 2]
**Difficulty:** 16[cite: 2]
**Potential Adversaries:** Elemental Spark, Lava Elemental (Greater Earth Elemental), Volcanic Dragon[cite: 2]

## FEATURES
* **Unstable Ground - Passive:** The ground here is uneven, prone to collapse, and constantly shifting[cite: 2]. PCs who fail an Agility Roll to move must mark a HP and are temporarily *Restrained*[cite: 2].
  * *What strange shapes and colors do the PCs see in the cooled lava? How do the people who live nearby keep their footing on the slick, shiny surface?*[cite: 2]
* **Violent Eruption - Action:** Spend a Fear to have the ground erupt at a point of your choice, spewing lava into the air and spreading it along the ground[cite: 2]. All targets within Very Close range of that point lose a Hope and take 3d8+3 physical damage[cite: 2]. The area within Very Close range of the eruption point becomes lava until the end of the scene[cite: 2]. A creature who moves into or through the lava takes 3d8+3 physical damage[cite: 2]. Until the end of the scene, you can spend a Fear to expand the lava area by one step (such as Very Close to Close or Close to Far)[cite: 2].
  * *What makes the lava seem alive? How can the PCs predict its behavior?*[cite: 2]
* **Vent Toxic Gas - Action:** Spend a Fear to open a fissure that belches out toxic gas at a point of your choice, creating a cloud that blankets everything within Far range[cite: 2]. While inside the cloud, a PC must track their air supply with an Air Supply Countdown[cite: 2]. Its starting value is equal to 3 + their tier, and it ticks down when they take an action[cite: 2]. If a PC fails an action roll or rolls with Fear, you can use a GM move to tick down their countdown an additional time[cite: 2]. If the roll is a failure with Fear, you can tick it down twice instead[cite: 2]. Once the countdown ends, a PC must mark a Stress whenever they take an action within the gas until they escape the cloud[cite: 2].
  * *What signs do you see to indicate the presence of toxic gas? What taste lingers in the mouths of the PCs who inhale the gas?*[cite: 2]
