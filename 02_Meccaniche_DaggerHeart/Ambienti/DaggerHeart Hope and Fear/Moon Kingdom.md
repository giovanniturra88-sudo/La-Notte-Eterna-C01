# Moon Kingdom
*Tier 4 Exploration*
*The mysterious, luminous, and isolated ruins of a long-lost lunar kingdom that echoes with forgotten magic and ancient technology.*

**Impulses:** Blur the line between dream and reality, let consciousness ebb and flow like the tide, whisper echoes of ancient opulence[cite: 3]
**Difficulty:** 20[cite: 3]
**Potential Adversaries:** Local Fauna (Cryptimoths, Yufos), Moon Queen's Spirit (Arch-Necromancer), Reawakened Relics (Constructs, Vault Guardians)[cite: 3]

## FEATURES
* **Dreamlike Atmosphere - Passive:** Everything in the Moon Kingdom feels dreamlike and floaty[cite: 3]. A leap carries you far, and the sound is muted[cite: 3]. The air is full of an iridescent crystalline dust that shifts in response to thoughts or emotions and might record memories of the past or visions of the future[cite: 3]. Once per scene, a PC can make an Instinct Roll to read the psychic impressions left by past events[cite: 3].
  * *What ethereal scents or sensations do the PCs experience? How does the atmosphere affect the interactions between them?*[cite: 3]
* **Silent Ruins - Passive:** The towering silver and blue architecture of the capital is undamaged but overgrown with bioluminescent flora that pulses in time with the lunar tides[cite: 3]. A PC who succeeds on a Finesse Roll can harvest a moon plant without killing it[cite: 3]. A PC who eats one or more living moon plants gains a +1 bonus to Spellcast Rolls until their next long rest[cite: 3].
  * *What earth flora do these moon plants look like? What do they taste like, and how do they affect the eater physically?*[cite: 3]
* **Lunar Astrologum - Passive:** Atop the highest tower in the capital city sits a crystal dream machine that can replay history[cite: 3]. A PC who succeeds on a Knowledge Roll can repair the machine[cite: 3]. Once the machine is repaired, a PC who succeeds on a Presence Roll can operate it to learn the history of the Moon Kingdom's rise and fall[cite: 3].
  * **Critical Success:** The PC receives clear information about key events and figures in the Moon Kingdom's history and gains 1d4 Hope, which they can distribute among PCs they share their visions with[cite: 3].
  * **Success with Hope:** The PC receives clear information about key events and figures in the Moon Kingdom's history[cite: 3].
  * **Any roll with Fear:** The PC receives brief flashes and sensations related to the kingdom's cataclysmic end[cite: 3]. If the roll was a failure, the PC takes 4d10+5 magic damage as the visions assault their psyche[cite: 3].
  * *What does this machinery look like, and how does it work? What do the PCs learn about the Moon Kingdom's history?*[cite: 3]
* **The Moon Queen's Tomb - Reaction:** The Moon Kingdom's final ruler has been preserved in a selenite coffin deep within the citadel[cite: 3]. On a roll with Fear to investigate her chamber, her baleful shade awakens and attacks the party[cite: 3]. Summon the Moon Queen's Spirit (Arch-Necromancer) next to her coffin and immediately spotlight her[cite: 3].
  * *Who buried her here, and why? Can she be resurrected, or is destroying her spirit the only path forward?*[cite: 3]
