# Megastorm
*Tier 3 Event*
*An extreme weather event suddenly manifests, forcing the party to balance their own survival against the safety of others.*

**Impulses:** Blow hurricane winds, choke with mudslides, crumble structures, flood, throw debris, light up the sky[cite: 1]
**Difficulty:** 16[cite: 1]
**Potential Adversaries:** Greater Water Elemental, Storm Titan[cite: 1]

## FEATURES
* **Stormfront - Passive:** Countdown (8). When the storm arrives, activate the countdown. It ticks down when a PC rolls with Hope. When it triggers, the storm disperses or moves on to a different area[cite: 1].
  * *Where is the safest place to weather the storm? What are the people here willing to risk their lives for?*[cite: 1]
* **Hazardous Conditions - Passive:** The Megastorm's extreme wind, fog, and cloud cover make navigating the entire area difficult[cite: 1]. PCs can't move as part of an action roll without succeeding on an Agility Reaction Roll[cite: 1].
  * *Who else lost in the gyre? How can you lead them out?*[cite: 1]
* **Bomb Cyclone - Action:** Spend a Fear to summon an extreme weather event, such as a thunderstorm or tornado, at a point of your choice[cite: 1]. Each target within Close range of that point must succeed on a reaction roll using a trait you choose to avoid being pummeled by hail, thrown into the air, blasted by lightning, or a similar effect[cite: 1]. Targets who fail take 2d10+2 damage of a type you choose[cite: 1].
  * *Is this the worst weather any of the PCs have experienced? What in the immediate area can the PCs use to protect themselves?*[cite: 1]
* **Stormheart - Action:** Spend a Fear to reveal the living battery of the Megastorm[cite: 1]. Summon a Storm Giant or Greater Water Elemental within Far range of a PC, then immediately spotlight them[cite: 1]. Until this adversary is defeated, the "Stormfront" countdown doesn't tick down[cite: 1]. When this adversary is defeated, the Megastorm ends[cite: 1].
  * *Did this creature conjure the storm for their own purposes, or do they serve an even greater entity? Why did they designate this place for destruction?*[cite: 1]
* **Panic in the Streets - Action:** The Megastorm is sowing fear and chaos among the locals[cite: 1]. Describe how the storm puts an NPC in danger[cite: 1]. If the PC who next takes the spotlight doesn't use their action to rescue the endangered NPC, you gain a Fear[cite: 1].
  * *Who's taking advantage of the situation? Who's supposed to be in charge here, and what happened to them?*[cite: 1]
