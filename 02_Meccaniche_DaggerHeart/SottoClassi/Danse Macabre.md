# Danse Macabre

Play the Danse Macabre if you want to command the undead and manipulate death itself.

### SPELLCAST TRAIT

Undead

### FOUNDATION FEATURES

**_Death's Call:_** You can command one undead creature within 30 feet. The creature obeys your commands, but it must be able to see or hear you.

**_Soul Drain:_** When you hit with an attack, you can drain the target's life force. Roll a d6 and add it to your damage.

### SPECIALIZATION FEATURES

**_Undead Servants:_** You can command up to two undead creatures within 30 feet. The creatures obey your commands, but they must be able to see or hear you.

**_Graveyard Presence:_** When you deal damage with a spell or ability, the target takes an additional **1d6** necrotic damage.

### MASTERY FEATURES

**_Master of Death:_** When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points, you can mark an additional Hit Point on that creature.

**_Absolute Death:_** The additional necrotic damage from your "Graveyard Presence" feature increases to **2d6.**
