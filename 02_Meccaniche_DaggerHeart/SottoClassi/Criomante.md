# Criomante

Play the Criomante if you want to control ice and frost in your magic.

### SPELLCAST TRAIT

Gelo

### FOUNDATION FEATURES

**_Ice Touch:_** Your attacks deal an extra **1d6** cold damage.

**_Frozen Skin:_** You gain resistance to cold damage. When you take cold damage, reduce it by 5.

### SPECIALIZATION FEATURES

**_Icy Grip:_** Your attacks can cause the target to become restrained. On a hit, roll a d20. If the result is equal to or less than your proficiency bonus, the target becomes restrained until the end of their next turn.

**_Glacial Strike:_** The extra cold damage from your "Tocco di Ghiaccio" feature increases to **2d6**.

### MASTERY FEATURES

**_Frost Mastery:_** When you deal cold damage, you can mark an additional Hit Point on the target.

**_Absolute Gelo:_** The extra cold damage from your "Tocco di Ghiaccio" feature increases to **3d6.**
