# Cavaliere Predestinato

Play the Cavaliere Predestinato if you want to harness the power of fate and destiny in your magic.

### SPELLCAST TRAIT

Fate

### FOUNDATION FEATURES

**_Mark of Destiny:_** You've been touched by fate itself. Gain an additional Hit Point slot.

**_Fateful Strike:_** When you succeed with Fate on an attack roll, you deal an extra **1d10** magic damage.

### SPECIALIZATION FEATURES

**_Weaving Fate:_** You can manipulate the threads of destiny. While you have at least 2 Hope, you add your Proficiency to your Evasion.

**_Fate's Embrace:_** The extra magic damage from your "Fateful Strike" feature increases to **2d10**.

### MASTERY FEATURES

**_Master of Destiny:_** When you succeed on an attack, you can **mark a Stress** after rolling damage to force the target to mark an additional Hit Point.

**_Absolute Fate:_** The extra magic damage from your "Fateful Strike" feature increases to **3d10.**
