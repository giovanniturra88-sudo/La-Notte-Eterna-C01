# Maestro delle Meraviglie

Play the Maestro delle Meraviglie if you want to create wonders and marvels with your magic.

### SPELLCAST TRAIT

Arcane

### FOUNDATION FEATURES

**_Wonder Maker:_** You gain resistance to all damage types. When you take damage, reduce it by 5.

**_Marvelous Strike:_** Your attacks deal an extra **1d8** damage.

### SPECIALIZATION FEATURES

**_Incredible Creation:_** Once per turn, you can cause a target to become stunned. On a hit, roll a d20. If the result is equal to or less than your proficiency bonus, the target becomes stunned until the end of their next turn.

**_Marvelous Surge:_** The extra damage from your "Marvelous Strike" feature increases to **2d8**.

### MASTERY FEATURES

**_Master of Wonder:_** When you deal damage, you can mark an additional Hit Point on the target.

**_Absolute Marvel:_** The extra damage from your "Marvelous Strike" feature increases to **3d8.**
