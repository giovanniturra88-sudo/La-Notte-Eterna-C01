# Guardiano Nanico

Play the Guardiano Nanico if you want to use your small size and cunning to your advantage in battle.

### SPELLCAST TRAIT

Size

### FOUNDATION FEATURES

**_Nimble Escape:_** You gain a +2 bonus to your Dexterity saving throws.

**_Small but Mighty:_** Your attacks deal an extra **1d4** damage.

### SPECIALIZATION FEATURES

**_Quick Reflexes:_** When you succeed on a Dexterity saving throw, you can mark an additional Hit Point on the target.

**_Agile Strike:_** The extra damage from your "Small but Mighty" feature increases to **2d4**.

### MASTERY FEATURES

**_Master of Agility:_** When you deal damage with a spell or ability, you can mark an additional Hit Point on the target and force them to make a Dexterity saving throw or be knocked prone.

**_Absolute Agility:_** The extra damage from your "Small but Mighty" feature increases to **3d4.**
