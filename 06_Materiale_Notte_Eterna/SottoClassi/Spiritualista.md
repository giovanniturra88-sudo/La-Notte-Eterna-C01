# Spiritualista

Play the Spiritualista if you want to harness the power of necromantic magic.

### SPELLCAST TRAIT

Necromancy

### FOUNDATION FEATURES

**_Necrotic Touch:_** Your attacks deal an extra **1d6** necrotic damage.

**_Undead Resilience:_** You gain resistance to necrotic damage. When you take necrotic damage, reduce it by 5.

### SPECIALIZATION FEATURES

**_Soul Drain:_** Your attacks can cause the target to become weakened. On a hit, roll a d20. If the result is equal to or less than your proficiency bonus, the target has disadvantage on their next attack roll.

**_Death Mark:_** The extra necrotic damage from your "Necrotic Touch" feature increases to **2d6**.

### MASTERY FEATURES

**_Master of the Undead:_** When you deal necrotic damage, you can mark an additional Hit Point on the target.

**_Absolute Necromancy:_** The extra necrotic damage from your "Necrotic Touch" feature increases to **3d6**.
