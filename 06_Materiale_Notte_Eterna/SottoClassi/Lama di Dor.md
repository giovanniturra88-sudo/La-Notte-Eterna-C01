# Lama di Dor

Play the Lama di Dor if you want to channel divine energy through your sword.

### SPELLCAST TRAIT

Divine

### FOUNDATION FEATURES

**_Divine Strike:_** Your attacks deal an extra **1d8** radiant damage.

**_Sacred Armor:_** You gain resistance to radiant damage. When you take radiant damage, reduce it by 5.

### SPECIALIZATION FEATURES

**_Holy Weapon:_** Your attacks can cause the target to become frightened. On a hit, roll a d20. If the result is equal to or less than your proficiency bonus, the target becomes frightened until the end of their next turn.

**_Divine Surge:_** The extra radiant damage from your "Divine Strike" feature increases to **2d8**.

### MASTERY FEATURES

**_Master of Divinity:_** When you deal radiant damage, you can mark an additional Hit Point on the target.

**_Absolute Divinity:_** The extra radiant damage from your "Divine Strike" feature increases to **3d8.**
