# Legionario Xaan

Play the Legionario Xaan if you want to be a disciplined warrior of the Xaan Legion.

### SPELLCAST TRAIT

Martial

### FOUNDATION FEATURES

**_Tactical Mind:_** You gain resistance to all damage types. When you take damage, reduce it by 5.

**_Discipline:_** Your attacks deal an extra **1d8** damage.

### SPECIALIZATION FEATURES

**_Formation Strike:_** Once per turn, you can cause a target to become prone. On a hit, roll a d20. If the result is equal to or less than your proficiency bonus, the target becomes prone until the end of their next turn.

**_Legion Surge:_** The extra damage from your "Discipline" feature increases to **2d8**.

### MASTERY FEATURES

**_Master of Tactics:_** When you deal damage, you can mark an additional Hit Point on the target.

**_Absolute Legion:_** The extra damage from your "Discipline" feature increases to **3d8.**
